Selected Work

High-ticket education, sold on the phone

National accredited training provider (RTO) · $3k to $12.5k qualifications, booked careers calls

Problem: Qualifications sold through consultation calls, with a stalled pipeline, rising cost per lead, landing pages dragging quality score, and spend leaking to search terms that never converted.

What I did: On search, I rebuilt the account structure and ad copy, shipped ad-group-level landing pages, and cut the waste. On Meta, I built a creative-led prospecting and retargeting engine feeding the same careers-call funnel, with a constant testing cadence of hooks, angles and formats. I also stood up an AI agent that tracks competitor course offers and promotions in near real time, so we could counter-position pricing and intake angles within the day, not the month. Across both channels, CRM enrolment data was wired back into bidding, so the algorithms optimised to booked calls and closed enrolments, not form fills.

Result: Blended cost per acquisition held near $100, and attributed deal revenue more than quadrupled in a single month as the turnaround took hold.
