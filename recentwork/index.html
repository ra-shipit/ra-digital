Selected Work

01

Full-funnel growth for a high-ticket education provider

National accredited training provider (RTO) · $3k to $12.5k qualifications, booked careers calls

Problem

Qualifications sold through consultation calls, with a stalled pipeline, rising cost per lead, landing pages dragging quality score, and spend leaking to search terms that never converted.

What I did

On search, I rebuilt the account structure and ad copy, shipped ad-group-level landing pages, and cut the waste. On Meta, I built a creative-led prospecting and retargeting engine feeding the same careers-call funnel, with a constant testing cadence of hooks, angles and formats. I also stood up an AI agent that tracks competitor course offers and promotions in near real time, so we could counter-position pricing and intake angles within the day, not the month. To lift lead quality, I reworked the forms with qualifying questions and built an email nurture sequence that warmed enquiries into booked calls. Across both channels, CRM enrolment data was wired back into bidding, so the algorithms optimised to booked calls and closed enrolments, not form fills.

Result

Blended cost per acquisition held near $100, lead quality and booked-call rates improved, and attributed deal revenue more than doubled as the turnaround took hold.

02

Value-based bidding across a card portfolio

Tier-1 finance client · $80M+ media account · SA360, value-based bidding

Problem

The client ran four to five credit card products, and the priority shifted month to month: the card the business most wanted to grow kept changing. Standard cost-per-acquisition bidding treated every card the same and could not steer demand toward the one that mattered most.

What I did

Leading a team of seven on the account, I moved the card portfolio onto value-based bidding. Working with the client, we set a conversion value for each card and reallocated those values as priorities shifted, so the algorithm leaned into the cards the business wanted and eased off the rest. Bidding moved from cost per acquisition to conversion value, with the value weights as the steering wheel.

Result

More applications on the priority cards, with cost per acquisition coming down and conversion volume up across the portfolio.

03

From a HK$450K target to a seven-figure day

Premium DTC meat brand, Hong Kong · eCommerce launch

Brief

Hit a HK$450K single-day revenue target on a flagship sale launch.

What I did

Built a manufactured-scarcity hype sequence around one hero SKU, capped at a limited run, sequenced across teaser, VIP early access and launch emails alongside paid. That single SKU drove roughly a third of the day's revenue and pulled hundreds of other products into carts.

Result

A HK$1.03M day from 825 orders with zero cancellations, the brand's first seven-figure sales day in five years and more than 2x the target. The all-time sales-day record has been broken four times since the engagement began.
